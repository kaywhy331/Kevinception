# Changelog

## 0.8.0

- Replaced floating black-void display stands with connected physical era dioramas.
- Added a continuous timeline corridor and visible neighboring-era portals.
- Expanded 1990 into a warm living-room scene with a grounded television, console, controller, media, lighting, and cables.
- Expanded 2000 into a personal computer bedroom with a deep CRT, tower, modem, peripherals, CD stack, corkboard, Xanga printout, and desk clutter.
- Expanded 2010 into a bright connected workspace with laptop, smartphone, camera, photos, headphones, plant, and natural light.
- Expanded 2020 into a creator studio with phone rig, ring light, microphone boom, editing station, acoustic panels, and reactive content particles.
- Rebuilt 2030 as a physical Autonomous Systems Lab with agent stations, a mission table, memory archive, evidence panels, and human approval console.
- Rebuilt 2040 as a physical Continuity Sanctuary with holographic Kevin, memory columns, thought interpreter, reflective pool, biophilic elements, and luminous architecture.
- Physically connected 2030 and 2040 with observation glass and an animated data conduit.
- Reduced the large era cards to compact timeline docks with expandable descriptions.
- Refined environment and interface camera poses so the future-wing connection is visible.

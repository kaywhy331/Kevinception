About Kevinception

The portfolio is the work.
The interface is the argument.

Kevinception follows the technology interfaces that shaped Kevin’s curiosity—from television and games to the internet, social networks, short-form media, autonomous agents, and digital identity.

K

Origin signal

Curiosity connected first.

Growing up in the 1990s, Kevin was instantly hooked by the early internet. Dial-up tones became the soundtrack to countless hours exploring online communities, designing ASCII art chatroom scrollers, experimenting with punter bots and small scripts, and discovering that technology could turn imagination into an interactive world.

By 2000, curiosity had become a durable passion for technology, systems, design, automation, and invention. That same pattern still drives his work: explore widely, recognize the underlying structure, and convert possibility into something people can actually use.

Why these years?

Nostalgia is not the product. Recognition is.

Each world should evoke a defining technology moment without depending on copied logos, interfaces, audio, or trademarks. The goal is to recreate the emotional grammar: how people discovered, created, connected, and imagined the future.

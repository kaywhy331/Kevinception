# Kevinception V7.1 — Connected Environment Art Direction

**Release:** 0.8.0  
**Status:** Implemented physical-environment pass

## Product correction

The six eras are no longer presented as isolated devices on floating shelves. They now form a continuous sequence of inhabitable technology environments:

```text
1990 Living Room
  → 2000 Computer Bedroom
  → 2010 Connected Workspace
  → 2020 Creator Studio
  → 2030 Permissioned Smart Home
  → 2040 Continuity Sanctuary
```

The camera remains authored. Visitors move between stations through the timeline controls rather than navigating a free-roaming 3D museum.

## Environment requirements now represented

Each era includes:

- A floor, walls, ceiling, and environmental depth
- Era-specific lighting and material direction
- A primary technology device
- Supporting props that reinforce Kevin's story
- An establishing camera and a focused interface camera
- A visible architectural continuation toward adjacent eras
- Physical interaction points tied to functional applications

## Shared future wing

2030 and 2040 are deliberately modeled as adjacent physical spaces.

### 2030 — Permissioned Smart Home

The apartment contains:

- Distinct local-sensing kitchen, mounted-context studio, guest-safe living, and private-threshold zones
- Five human-scale objects that carry the day from a morning mug to after-gathering glasses
- A room-scale Wren presence rather than a humanoid assistant or command-center dashboard
- A five-node decision rail that makes Sense → Interpret → Authority → Act or wait → Receipt visible in the architecture
- A marked human-authority gate and consent state linked to the active object
- Quiet, inspectable infrastructure receipts for provenance and retention boundaries
- Observation glass into the 2040 chamber

### 2040 — Continuity Sanctuary

The sanctuary contains:

- A holographic Kevin dais
- Orbiting memory shards
- Memory archive columns
- A thought interpreter console
- Reflective water or glass flooring
- Biophilic elements
- A visible connection back to the 2030 lab

### Physical transition

The selected 2030 moment and its explicit keep/refuse state move through a visible animated conduit toward the 2040 sanctuary. The transition is therefore a bridge between two real places and two real state models, not a replacement for either environment.

## Current art-production boundary

The release establishes spatial composition, materials, props, lighting, interaction zones, and continuity with procedural R3F geometry. A later production pass can selectively replace hero devices and props with optimized artist-authored GLB assets without changing the experience architecture.
